Get help

Something not working, or a question about your account? Email 115362496@umail.ucc.ie and we will get back to you, usually within a day or two.

To report a listing, a farm, or a conversation, use the Report option inside the app — on a listing page, a farm page, or the menu in a conversation. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

How do I reset my password?
Tap “Forgot password?” on the sign-in screen. The link in the email opens in your browser.
How do I stop seeing someone?
Block them from their farm page or from your conversation. Undo it any time under Profile → Blocked Accounts.
I signed up as a buyer but want to sell.
Profile → Become a seller. This is one-way — a seller account cannot switch back.
How do I delete my account?
Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, listings, messages, and photos.
Does FarmStand handle payments?
No. Buyers pay sellers directly, in person or through the seller’s own payment handle. FarmStand never processes money and charges no fees.
